## Goal
Remove what hurts daily dwmt use most: a run that stalls unnoticed, wasted repair rounds on a missing environment, long reports and UAT steps that repeat tests, a full suite of unrelated modules at finish, and PO commands that half-write. Context: `roadmapa-dwmt.md` step K5 (report, UAT, suite by modules; the price from history is not in this sprint), `docs/zgloszenie-2026-10-20/ideas.md` §0.8, §0.9, and `bugs.txt` pts 17-20, 24 in the dwmt-master plugin copy (the items carry them).

## Acceptance
- AC-1 A task session started in the background that writes no first step within a few minutes, or ends on an API/network error, is relaunched once; a second failure stops the run with a push. `product brief`, `watch` and `supervise status` show such a run as stalled (B-57). Check first which part still applies with the driver on by default.
- AC-2 Reading run state does not change the run: no session added, no task marked started (B-58).
- AC-3 A verify command whose program is not found (cmd 9009, missing executable) gives `result: env`, never red tests routed to the executor (B-59).
- AC-4 The final report opens with at most 10 plain lines in the configured `language`: what changed for the product, what was not done and why, what was deferred, what the human has to do, cost against the price. Identifiers only below, always with a description. The header gives one status per criterion, no glued strings.
- AC-5 UAT steps come only from risks and criteria that need a human (judgement, decision, screen); risks proved by `test`, `guard` or `investigate` make none. A sprint with no such steps records its UAT as passed at finish and closes in the roadmap; the summary says so; late defects stay possible.
- AC-6 The full suite at finish runs the tests of the modules the sprint changed plus cross-module contract tests. A known red test has an expiry (default 7 days, configurable) and an owner backlog item created with it; after expiry it blocks `finish` again.
- AC-7 A rejected `spec fix` (or any product command refused by a limit) writes nothing (B-54). `spec fix` writes a one-sentence goal and one criterion "each listed item resolved", not every title three times; sprints `done` do not count toward the roadmap byte budget (B-55).
- AC-8 `start-check` prices a fix sprint from finished fix sprints (or per item), not from feature-sprint analogs (B-56).

## Out of scope

- Review remarks from earlier sprints (a separate fix sprint), steps K6-K10.
